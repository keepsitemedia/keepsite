# Keepsite Media rebrand design

**Status:** approved in chat 2026-09-13
**Branch:** `rebrand`, based on `office-additions`
**Supersedes** the visual identity in `2026-08-23-keepsite-brand-transition.md` (green and clay palette, Instrument Sans and Newsreader, Memphis background shapes). Copy, structure, and page set are unchanged.

## 1. Source

The logo lives at `docs/brand/keepsitelogo.png` (1440 by 862, white background). It has four parts:

| Part | Face | Color |
|---|---|---|
| Stripe block, three stacked bands | | slate `#628997`, mustard `#DFAA3F`, rust `#B8512C` |
| "Keepsite" | Arial Bold | black |
| Heavy horizontal rule under the wordmark | | black |
| "media", right-aligned under "ite", overlapping the rule | Georgia Italic | copper `#D1884B` |
| Tagline, all caps, wide tracking | Montserrat Bold | black |

The whole site follows from those four parts. The office (`/office/`) is out of scope: it keeps building against the new tokens but is not restyled.

Rules:

- Slate, mustard, and copper never carry text and never sit under white text.

- `--color-brand-tint` and `--color-accent` are removed. Anything that used them is listed in section 6.

## 3. Type

Three roles, one per part of the logo.

| Token | Stack | Weights | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--font-sans` | `Arial, 'Arimo', 'Liberation Sans', Helvetica, sans-serif` | 400, 700 | body, headings, nav |
| `--font-serif` | `Georgia, 'Gelasio', 'Times New Roman', serif` | 400 italic | the `.serif` accent |
| `--font-label` | `'Montserrat', Arial, 'Arimo', sans-serif` | 700 | buttons, nav links, eyebrows, footer tagline |

- Arimo and Gelasio are the metric-compatible open clones of Arial and Georgia. They are declared through `@fontsource/arimo` (Apache 2.0) and `@fontsource/gelasio` (OFL) but sit after the system face in the stack, so a device with Arial and Georgia installed fetches nothing. Android and Linux fetch them and get an identical layout. The old metric-fallback `@font-face` block goes away because the fallback is now the same design.
- Montserrat 700 latin (`@fontsource/montserrat/700.css`) is the one file every visitor downloads. It is preloaded in place of Instrument Sans.
- Headings: `--font-sans` 700, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`, `line-height: 1.05`. Body 400 at `--step-0`, `line-height: 1.6`. `strong` is 700.
- `.label`: `--font-label` 700, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.14em`, `font-size: 0.8125rem`. Buttons and nav links take it.
- `.serif`: `--font-serif` 400 italic, `letter-spacing: 0`, color rust. This lifts the 2026-08-29 no-italics rule for the serif accent only. Body and headings stay upright.
- `@fontsource-variable/instrument-sans` and `@fontsource-variable/newsreader` are uninstalled. `OfficeLayout.astro` imports the new packages instead.

## 4. Logo, favicon, share image

**`src/components/Logo.astro`**, HTML and CSS, not SVG text, so the wordmark uses the page's font stack and reads as text to assistive tech. Two variants by prop:

- `compact` (header): stripe block, "Keepsite" in `--font-sans` 700, "media" in `--font-serif` italic copper on the same baseline. One line, about 28px tall.
- `full` (footer): stripe block and wordmark, the heavy rule under the wordmark, "media" right-aligned beneath the rule, and the tagline in `.label` under everything.

The stripe block is an inline SVG of three rects. The link text for both variants is "Keepsite Media, home".

**`public/favicon.svg`**: the stripe block alone, three bands, no rounding. **`public/apple-touch-icon.png`**: same at 180px, generated by `scripts/make-icons.mjs` with sharp. `BaseLayout.astro` links both.

**`public/og-default.png`**: `scripts/make-og-image.mjs` composites `docs/brand/keepsitelogo.png` onto a white 1200 by 630 canvas with sharp, so the card is pixel-exact and needs no fonts on the build machine.

The PNG is not served from `public/`.

## 5. Ornament

`BackgroundShapes.astro` is deleted. The logo's two devices replace it.

- **Stripe block** (`.stripe`, three bands, 18 by 42px): once above the h1 on every page, and once on the closing band above its heading.
- **Rule** (`.rule`): a 3px `--color-ink` line. It is the header's bottom edge and the divider above every section h2, drawn at 3rem wide. Sections otherwise separate by whitespace and the alternating band.
- **Tier bar**: each package card gets a 6px top border. Presence slate, Growth mustard, Agile rust, Range copper. The same colors mark the tiers nowhere else.
- **Buttons**: `.btn` is ink with white text, rust on hover. `.btn-outline` is an ink border with ink text, ink fill and white text on hover. Both use `.label` type. `.link-arrow` is rust with a 2px underline.
- **Closing band** (`.section-deep`): ink background, white text, heading in `.serif` white, button mustard with ink text, mustard darkens to `#C9962F` on hover.
- **Header**: white, sticky, the rule as its bottom edge. Active nav link is rust with a 2px rust underline.
- **Footer**: `--color-surface-alt`, hairline top, full logo.
- **Focus ring**: two-tone stays. Outer ring ink on white surfaces, white on the closing band.
- `html` background is white and `body` gets no background, as before.
