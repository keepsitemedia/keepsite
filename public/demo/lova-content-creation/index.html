Round two — four directions, one brand

Lova Content Creation, four ways.

All four start from the same place this time: one person who marries you, photographs it, films it and brings the flowers, anywhere from the Uintas to the red rock. They differ in how they tell it. Scroll through all four and tell me which feels most like you. Taking the type from one and the colour from another is allowed and encouraged.

Heads up There are still no photographs in here on purpose. Every picture is drawn as an outlined box, sized to the shape the real photo will fill and labelled with what belongs there. Your own photos and video go in once a direction is picked, and they will do most of the talking. The illustrations are drawn in the page, not stock.
