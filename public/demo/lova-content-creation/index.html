Four directions — one brand

Lova Content Creation, four ways.

Four different feels for the same home page. Same words, same sections — each one carries a completely different mood. Scroll through all four, then tell me which one feels most like you. Taking the type from one and the colour from another is allowed and encouraged.

Heads up There are no photographs in here on purpose. Every picture is drawn as an outlined box, sized to the shape the real photo will fill and labelled with what belongs there — a vertical clip, a detail shot, a portrait. That keeps this round about the design instead of about which photo you like. Your photos go in once a direction is picked. Copy is real, pulled from your questionnaire.
