Four directions

Lova Content Creation, four ways

All four start from your logo and your voice guide: the lily, the cream and olive, and “You live it. I’ll pay attention.” Your logo stays the same in every one, and the words are written to sound like you. What changes is how the page is built around them. Scroll through all four and tell me which feels most like you. Taking the type from one and the colour from another is allowed and encouraged.

Heads up There are no photographs in here on purpose. Every picture is drawn as a plain box, sized to the shape the real photo or video will fill and labelled with what belongs there. The lily is lifted from the Canva preview of your logo, so it is a little soft at large sizes; the full file replaces it. Every palette is built to hold up with colour blindness: nothing depends on telling red from green, and all text meets the AA contrast standard.
